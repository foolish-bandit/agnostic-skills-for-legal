# Skill: Case Assessment

Preliminary assessment of litigation matters.

## Workflow
1.  **Factual Summary:** Extract the key facts and timeline from the provided documents.
2.  **Legal Theories:** Identify potential causes of action or defenses.
3.  **Risk Analysis:** Evaluate the strength of the case based on available evidence.
4.  **Action Items:** Suggest immediate next steps (e.g., litigation hold, expert witness search).
