# Skill: Trademark Clearance

Structured review for trademark availability and risk.

## Workflow
1.  **Mark Description:** Describe the proposed mark and associated goods/services.
2.  **Likelihood of Confusion:** Search for similar marks in the same or related classes.
3.  **Descriptiveness:** Assess if the mark is generic, descriptive, suggestive, or arbitrary.
4.  **Risk Level:** Assign a risk level (Low/Med/High) for registration and use.
