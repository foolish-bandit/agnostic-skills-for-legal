# Skill: AI Risk Assessment

Structured assessment for AI systems and models.

## Workflow
1.  **System Description:** Identify the AI system's purpose, inputs, and outputs.
2.  **Risk Classification:** Determine if the system is "High Risk," "Limited Risk," or "Minimal Risk" (per EU AI Act or similar).
3.  **Bias & Fairness:** Evaluate potential for biased outcomes or discriminatory impact.
4.  **Transparency:** Check for user disclosure and system documentation.
5.  **Mitigation:** Suggest technical or organizational controls to reduce risk.
