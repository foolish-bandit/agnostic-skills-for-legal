# Skill: Employee Complaint Intake & Triage

## When to use this skill
Use this to document an initial employee complaint (e.g., harassment, discrimination, wage theft) and determine the urgency of the response.

## Trigger phrases
- "An employee made a complaint"
- "Log a workplace grievance"
- "Triage a harassment claim"

## Required user inputs
- Complainant name and role.
- Respondent(s) name and role.
- Description of the incident(s).
- Dates and potential witnesses.

## Workflow
1. **Fact Capture:** Log the who, what, where, and when.
2. **Issue Categorization:** Identify if the claim involves protected classes, wage/hour violations, or policy breaches.
3. **Interim Measures:** Suggest immediate steps (e.g., separation of parties, paid leave).
4. **Urgency Level:** Assign a priority based on the severity of the allegations.

## Output format: Intake Summary
- **Incident Summary:** Neutral, factual description of the claim.
- **Identified Risks:** Potential legal exposures (e.g., Title VII, Retaliation).
- **Recommended Interim Steps:** Actions to take within 24 hours.

## Guardrails
- **URGENT:** If the complaint involves physical violence or threats, escalate to Security and senior counsel immediately.
- Remind user: "Maintain strict confidentiality during intake."

## Example starter prompt
"An employee just reported that their manager is making inappropriate comments about their religion. Help me intake this complaint."
