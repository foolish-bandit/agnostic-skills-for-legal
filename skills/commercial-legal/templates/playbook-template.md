# Commercial Playbook Template

Use this template to define your organization's "standard" positions for negotiations.

## 1. Limitation of Liability
- **Standard:** 1x Annual Fees.
- **Fall-back:** 2x Annual Fees.
- **Walk-away:** No cap on liability.

## 2. Indemnification
- **Standard:** Mutual indemnification for 3rd party IP claims.
- **Fall-back:** One-way indemnification (if we are the provider).

## 3. Governing Law
- **Standard:** State of Delaware.
- **Fall-back:** State of New York.
