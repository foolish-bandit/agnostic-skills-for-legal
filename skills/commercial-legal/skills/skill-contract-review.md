# Skill: Contract Review

This skill provides a structured approach to reviewing commercial agreements.

## Workflow
1.  **Identify Agreement Type:** Determine if the document is an NDA, MSA, DPA, or other commercial contract.
2.  **Risk Assessment:** Scan for the following high-risk clauses:
    - Indemnification (unlimited liability)
    - Termination for convenience (lack of mutuality)
    - Governing law (unfavorable jurisdiction)
    - Data privacy obligations
3.  **Redline Recommendations:** Suggest specific edits to align the contract with standard market positions.
4.  **Summary Table:** Present findings in a table with columns: Clause, Issue, Risk Level (Low/Med/High), Proposed Edit.

## Tone
Professional, analytical, and risk-aware.
